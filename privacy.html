English

QUE Soft respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies only to the QUE Soft website. It does not automatically apply to Android applications or other products developed by QUE Soft. Separate privacy policies may be provided for those products when necessary.

2. Information You Provide

Our website does not currently provide user registration, account creation, or an online contact form. If you choose to contact us using the email link on this website, the information you include in your email, such as your email address and message, may be received and used to respond to your inquiry.

3. Technical Information and Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like other hosting providers, GitHub may process technical information and server logs when visitors access hosted websites. Such information may include IP addresses, browser details, request information, and access times, subject to GitHub's own policies and practices.

For more information, please review GitHub's Privacy Statement .

4. Cookies and Analytics

The current QUE Soft website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or third-party analytics tools. Hosting services or external links may operate under their own policies.

5. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. We do not control their content, security, or privacy practices. Please review the relevant policies when visiting those services.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not send personal information about a child unless you are authorized to do so.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable care when handling messages sent to us. However, no method of internet communication or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact QUE Soft at: quesoft0@gmail.com
